Product

TestPrintPage

A free printer test page utility. Print a test page to check your printer, no sign-up, no cost. Built by Derek Roga at Essence Technologies in Vilnius, Lithuania.

What it is

TestPrintPage is a free utility for checking a printer. You open the site, pick the sheet that matches what you are seeing, print it, and read the result. There is no account, no email gate, and no cost. The sheets are exact-size PDFs in A4 and US Letter, so what you print is what was designed, with no scaling surprises.

It launched in November 2026. Derek built it at Essence Technologies, the Vilnius company he founded and where he currently works, after noticing that most test-page tools start from a gallery of files instead of from the problem. TestPrintPage starts from the symptom: what you see on the page tells you which sheet to print, and the sheet tells you what is wrong.

The idea is simple, and it shapes the whole tool. A printer problem is something you see, a blank page, a faded photo, a colour that is missing, a line that should not be there. So the tool is organised around what you see, not around printer settings or technical terms. Each of the five sheets answers one question.

The five sheets

Each sheet is matched to a symptom. If you are not sure which one you need, start with the Quick sheet, then follow what it shows you.

Quick

One page that checks everything at a glance. Print this first when you are not sure what is wrong, or when you just want to know the printer is healthy.

Colour

Checks colour output: colour blocks, gradients and tone bars. Print it when colours look off, dull, or missing.

Black and White

Checks mono printing: grey steps, solid blacks and fine text. Print it when black text looks grey, fuzzy, or banded.

CMYK

Isolates each ink channel, cyan, magenta, yellow and black, so you can see exactly which one is failing instead of guessing.

Alignment

Checks print-head alignment. Print it when text looks blurry or doubled, or lines print offset from where they should be.

How to use it

  1. Print. Pick the sheet that matches your symptom and print it at 100 percent, with no scaling, on the paper size the sheet was made for. The guides on this site walk through the steps on Windows, Mac, and from the printer itself.
  2. Look. Compare the printed sheet with what it should show. Each guide on this site explains how to read its sheet, element by element, so you can name the fault instead of guessing at it.
  3. Fix. Follow the fix for the fault you found, then print the same sheet again. The second print is the proof: if the sheet looks right, the fix worked.

If you are starting from a symptom rather than a sheet, the printer help hub is the faster route. It matches blank pages, faded prints, wrong colours and streaks to the right guide and the right sheet in one click.

Exact-size PDFs, in A4 and US Letter

Every sheet is available as an exact-size PDF in both A4 and US Letter. That matters more than it sounds: a test page printed at the wrong size is scaled or cropped by the print dialog, and a scaled test page cannot be trusted. Lines move, margins shift, and the sheet stops measuring what it claims to measure. Pick the size that matches the paper in your tray, print at 100 percent, and the sheet is accurate. The A4 vs US Letter guide explains the difference and how to fix a size mismatch.
