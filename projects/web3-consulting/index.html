What Web3 consulting covers

Web3 consulting is, at its simplest, advice on whether and how to use blockchain technology. A consultant helps a team understand the options in plain language: what a chain can and cannot do, what it costs to build on one, and what the alternatives are. Much of the work is subtraction rather than addition: ruling out approaches that sound impressive and would waste months, and narrowing the choice to the one or two options that fit the actual problem.

Layer 2 networks are a common part of that conversation. A Layer 2 sits on top of a base chain like Ethereum and handles transactions off the main chain, which usually means lower fees and faster settlement, with the base chain still providing security. A zkEVM Layer 2 is one that runs smart contracts written for Ethereum and proves batches of transactions with zero-knowledge proofs, so existing Ethereum tools and contracts work on it without being rewritten.
