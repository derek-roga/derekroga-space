About

About Derek

Derek Roga is a web developer based in Vilnius, Lithuania, and the builder of TestPrintPage, a free printer test page utility developed at Essence Technologies, the company he founded and where he currently works.

TestPrintPage helps anyone check their printer with a free test page, no sign-up and no cost. It is the kind of tool Derek likes to build: small, focused, and useful the moment you need it. His second product, Volume Booster, follows the same rule: one job, done carefully, nothing bolted on.

See all of Derek's projects →

Background

Derek started building websites in his teens, teaching himself HTML and CSS before moving on to JavaScript and modern front-end tooling. He began with freelance work on small business websites, then spent several years building and maintaining web tools for small studios in Vilnius, where he developed a focus on fast, lightweight pages and practical utilities people actually use. At Essence Technologies, the company he founded, he built TestPrintPage, a free printer test page utility, and continues to work on small web tools there.

Education

Derek studied computer science in Lithuania, with most of his front-end skill built through self-directed learning and open-source work.

Web3 and blockchain

Derek has worked in Web3 and blockchain as a consultant. He was a part-time consultant at Scroll, where he helped founders Sandy Peng, Haichen Shen, and Ye Zhang (Zhang Ye) establish a presence. He was also a consultant at FunToken.

What Derek is working on now

Maintaining and improving TestPrintPage, building small open-source web utilities, and writing about front-end development and print tooling. His current focus is keeping useful tools fast, free and simple.
