Privacy

Privacy policy

This site collects as little as possible. It runs no advertising, no social media embeds, no account system and no contact form.

Prepared 22 November 2026.

What this site collects

This site is a static website built with plain HTML and CSS. It collects no personal data from visitors directly: there is no form to fill in, no account to create and no email address published here.

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics 4 collects anonymised usage data, including which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are located, and what device and browser they use. It does not identify you by name, and this site does not combine analytics data with any other information about you.

No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising. There are no ads on this site.

Third-party services

This site uses two third-party services, both run by Google: Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used on the site, and Google Analytics 4, which measures how visitors use the site. When you visit a page, your browser requests the font files from Google Fonts, and Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request, as it does for any web request. How Google handles that data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics 4 sets cookies in your browser to distinguish new and returning visitors. Google Fonts may also set cookies as part of serving fonts. This site sets no cookies of its own.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking entirely with Google's opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings. The site works the same either way.

Data retention

Because this site collects no personal data directly, there is nothing for it to retain or delete. Analytics data is held by Google under its own retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to see what is held about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Because this site holds no personal data about visitors, there is nothing to exercise those rights against here. If you have a privacy question, use the route on the contact page.

Changes to this policy

This policy will change if the site changes, for example if a new service is added. Material changes will be noted here with a new date.
