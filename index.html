TestPrintPage

A free printer test page utility. Print a test page to check your printer, no sign-up, no cost. Built by Derek Roga at Essence Technologies in Vilnius, Lithuania. It launched in November 2026.

TestPrintPage offers five test sheets, each matched to a symptom: Quick, Colour, Black and White, CMYK and Alignment, as exact-size A4 and US Letter PDFs. Print the sheet that matches what you see, and the sheet tells you what is wrong.
