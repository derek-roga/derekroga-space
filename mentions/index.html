About TestPrintPage

TestPrintPage is a free printer test page utility built by Derek Roga. It offers five symptom-matched test sheets, Quick, Colour, Black and White, CMYK and Alignment, as exact-size A4 and US Letter PDFs. Read more on the TestPrintPage project page, or see the printer help guides.

Derek Roga

Web developer in Vilnius, Lithuania. Builder of TestPrintPage and Volume Booster. Founder of Essence Technologies.
